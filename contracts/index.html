From opportunity to contract

Every step is reviewed by our team. Nothing is awarded automatically.

  1. 01OpportunityWe find or receive commercial cleaning work and qualify it: scope, access, schedule and economics.
  2. 02Published listingOnly a general summary is published. Client details stay private.
  3. 03ApplicationApproved providers who meet the requirements apply with their availability.
  4. 04SelectionWe review credentials, fit and pricing, and select one provider.
  5. 05Contract & work orderWork starts only after a signed agreement and an approved assignment.

A listing is not a contract. Applying does not guarantee work. An opportunity becomes paid work only after selection, a signed agreement and an approved work order.
